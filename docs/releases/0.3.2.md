# Spex Glance 0.3.2

A small cleanup release.

## Orders say when Kalshi cancelled them

If Kalshi cancels one of your resting orders itself (a market halt, a market closing, a change to
settlement bounds), the Orders tab now lists it under **Recently cancelled by Kalshi** with the
reason and the time. Orders you cancel yourself just disappear, as before. The list only covers
cancellations seen while the app was running and clears after 12 hours.

## Lighter on your API budget

Game schedules, team names and live scoreboards are public data on Kalshi, so the app now fetches
them without your key. Those requests no longer count against your account's API limit. Your
positions, orders, balance and settlements still go through your read-only key.

## Housekeeping

- The old `com.example` app group and keychain group, kept in 0.3.0 and 0.3.1 so settings could
  move across, are gone, as promised in the 0.3.0 notes. If you're updating straight from 0.2.x,
  you'll need to connect your Kalshi key again.
