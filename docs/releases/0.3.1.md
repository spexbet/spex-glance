# Spex Glance 0.3.1

## Live checkbox on Positions

There's a **Live** checkbox to the left of the sport pills. Tick it to see only games that have
started and haven't settled yet.

- A game in progress shows. So does a finished one marked *Final · awaiting settlement*, with its
  Won or Lost — it stays until Kalshi pays it out, then moves to Settled.
- A combo shows as soon as any one of its legs is live.
- It works with the sport pills: Live plus Hockey is live hockey only.
- It's remembered when you relaunch. If nothing's live, the list says so and offers **Show all**.

## Live means live

Earlier versions guessed when a game started, and for tennis the guess could be hours early: a
match that hadn't begun could show "LIVE · 5h in". The app now uses Kalshi's own game record:

- **Real start times** for every sport, so the clock and the "LIVE · 37m in" count are right, and
  the date on the card is the date where you are.
- **Not live until it's on.** Where Kalshi reports the game's status and keeps it current
  (baseball, basketball, football and most tennis), a game that hasn't started isn't live even if
  its scheduled time has passed. Where the status is stale, the start time decides.
- **Final sooner.** When Kalshi marks a game over, the card says *Final · awaiting settlement* right
  away instead of waiting for the market to close.

## Live scores

Expand a game on Positions and you get its scoreboard, straight from Kalshi's live feed:

- **Football:** score, quarter and clock, down and distance, who has the ball, the last play.
- **Basketball:** score, quarter (or half in college) and clock, possession.
- **Baseball:** score, top/bottom and inning, outs, the count, and a little diamond showing the runners.
- **Hockey:** score, period and clock, power plays.
- **Soccer:** score, minute, halftime, red cards, penalties.
- **Tennis:** sets, games in the current set, the point score (Deuce, Ad), and who's serving.
- **Cricket:** runs and wickets, and Kalshi's match status.

Collapsed cards show the score on one line, and the LIVE tag says where the game is ("LIVE · Q4 · 2:31",
"LIVE · Set 2 · 4–4"). Combo legs show their games' scores too, and so does the menu bar.

Scores refresh every 15 seconds while any of your games is on, with one request for all of them.
When none of your games is live, nothing is fetched. The same feed now decides when a game is live
or over, which is more reliable than the schedule alone.

## Clearer when something's wrong

- **Network problems say what to check.** Instead of Apple's raw "A server with the specified
  hostname could not be found", the footer now says things like *Can't look up Kalshi's address —
  check your VPN or DNS*.
- **The Kalshi Health dot follows the last attempt.** If the last refresh couldn't get out of your
  Mac, the dot goes amber and the line says why; it clears on the next one that gets through.
  A rejected key turns it red. The dot and the footer line now always agree, and Kalshi isn't
  blamed for your VPN.
- **Empty lists explain themselves.** No positions at all, only non-sports positions, nothing live
  right now, or a key that has lost *Read all data* — each gets its own message and what to do.

## Work Mode shows no money

With Work Mode on, the app shows no money at all — just the games, their status and score, and
each position's chance. That covers Positions (no value, payout, cost or P&L), Orders (no size or
limit price), Settled (Won/Lost only) and the menu bar dropdown.

## Your API tier

Settings → About now shows your account's Kalshi API tier (Basic, Advanced, Expert and up). It's
one request at most per launch. Spex Glance uses a small fraction of even the Basic tier.

## A fresh coat for the menu bar and widget

- The menu bar dropdown matches the window: Cash / Positions / Total tiles, one card per game with
  its status and each position's chance, and the window's fonts and colors. Work Mode now drops
  the tiles and the dollar signs here too.
- The small widget is a cobalt tile with your unrealized P&L. Medium and large use the same game
  lines as the app, with LIVE and Final marked. Every size says "as of" so nobody mistakes it for
  live.

## Smaller things

- New Kalshi API keys made on Kalshi's website are now Ed25519 by default. They import exactly
  like the old RSA ones; the setup guide and the connect wizard say so.
- A guard for Kalshi's new "floor" settlement markets: if one ever turns up in sports, it shows
  *Settled* instead of a Won/Lost the app can't be sure of. None exist in sports today.
- New DISCLAIMER.md in the repository, plus troubleshooting entries for VPNs and the Live checkbox.
