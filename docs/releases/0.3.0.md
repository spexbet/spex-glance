# Spex Glance 0.3.0

## Four sections, four shortcuts

The window is now split into Positions, Orders, Settled and P&L. Press ⌘1 to ⌘4 to jump between them, or ⌘⇧[ and ⌘⇧] to cycle. The money tiles and the status line stay put above all four.

- **Positions** is the view you know. Nothing changed there.
- **Orders** holds your resting orders, moved out from under the positions list into their own section. It still only shows them — Spex Glance never places, changes or cancels an order.
- **Settled** lists every sports market Kalshi has settled for you, newest first, grouped by day with the day's net. Each row shows Won or Lost, what it made or lost, and the fee. A sport filter and a fees checkbox sit at the top.
- **P&L** charts it. The realized line is rebuilt from Kalshi's own settlement records every time you open the app, so it's correct from the first launch and nothing is stored for it. Pick 1D, 7D, 30D or All; untick "Include fees" to see gross.

Work Mode hides the P&L section entirely and drops the dollar signs from Orders and Settled.

## Live Line (optional)

Kalshi has no history of what your portfolio was worth over time, so if you want that line, the app has to record it. Live Line is off until you turn it on in Settings → Charts.

- When it's on, the app samples your sports total about once a minute while prices move and draws it on the P&L section.
- Turning it on asks where to keep the history. **This Mac only** keeps it encrypted inside the app on this computer; it exists nowhere else, and Disconnect erases it. **iCloud** also syncs it between your Macs through your private iCloud storage, which Apple encrypts. If iCloud isn't available, the history stays on this Mac and syncs once it is.
- Your Kalshi key is never part of what's stored or synced.
- "Save as CSV…" on the card exports every sample.
- The history is kept at full detail for a day, then thinned to one point per five minutes for a week and one per hour after that.

## Your settings now actually stick

A bug in every earlier version meant the app's settings — Work Mode, sport filter, sort order, menu bar choices — quietly reset whenever the app relaunched. Fixed. Because of the same fix, this update moves the app to a new identity under the hood, and two things need a one-time redo:

- **Launch at login** will show as off. Turn it back on in Settings → Menu bar if you had it.
- **The widget** needs to be removed and added again in Notification Center.

Your Kalshi key carries over on its own; you won't need to reconnect.

## Under the hood

- Settled markets come from Kalshi's settlements endpoint, pulled incrementally after the first load.
- Live Line iCloud sync uses CloudKit's private database directly: one record per Mac per hour, so two Macs never conflict.
- App Group and keychain group are now `<TeamID>.bet.spex.glance`; the old `com.example` ones are read once to migrate and will be dropped in the next release.
