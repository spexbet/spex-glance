# Spex Glance 0.3.3

A small cleanup release.

## Orders say when Kalshi cancelled them

If Kalshi cancels one of your resting orders itself (a market halt, a market closing, a change to
settlement bounds), the Orders tab now lists it under **Recently cancelled by Kalshi** with the
reason and the time. Orders you cancel yourself just disappear, as before. The list only covers
cancellations seen while the app was running and clears after 12 hours.

## Widget taps open Glance, once

Clicking the widget used to open Kalshi in your browser and a new Spex Glance window every
time. Now:

- Clicking a game on the medium or large widget brings up the Spex Glance window on Positions,
  with that game expanded and scrolled into view. If a filter would have hidden it, the filter
  steps aside.
- Clicking anywhere else on the widget (or the small widget) opens the window on Positions.
- There's only ever one window. Clicking again brings the same one forward.
- Kalshi is still one click away with **Open Kalshi portfolio**.

## Lighter on your API budget

Game schedules, team names and live scoreboards are public data on Kalshi, so the app now fetches
them without your key. Those requests no longer count against your account's API limit. Your
positions, orders, balance and settlements still go through your read-only key.

## Live Line on a second Mac

Install Spex Glance on another Mac signed into the same iCloud account (a work machine, say),
turn on Live Line with iCloud, and it now downloads the whole line before it records anything,
so both Macs show the same history.

- A Mac with no Live Line history of its own always does a full download first.
- If a download is cut short, it tries again within a minute instead of waiting 15 minutes.
- The chart's "since" date is now the oldest point you have, including what came from your other Macs.
- Fixed a loop that could fire a burst of iCloud requests after a settings change.

## Housekeeping

- The old `com.example` app group and keychain group, kept in 0.3.0 through 0.3.2 so settings could
  move across, are gone, as promised in the 0.3.0 notes. If you're updating straight from 0.2.x,
  you'll need to connect your Kalshi key again.
- A redrawn Spex grin on the app icon and in the menu bar: sharper, with a flatter arrow.
